The integer polynomial displayed by the companion HTML reader is
\begin{equation}
  Z(X)=\sum_{i=0}^{56} z_iX^i\in\mathbf Z[X].
\end{equation}
Its leading coefficient is used as the common denominator:
\begin{equation}
  D=z_{56}>0,
  \qquad
  f(X)=\frac{Z(X)}{D}\in\mathbf Q[X].
\end{equation}
All odd coefficients vanish.  Hence, on setting
\begin{equation}
  P_{28}(T)=\sum_{j=0}^{28}\frac{z_{2j}}{D}T^j,
\end{equation}
we have the exact identity
\begin{equation}
  f(X)=P_{28}(X^2).
\end{equation}

The canonical coefficient file is \texttt{f56\_Z.txt}.  Its coefficient
order is ascending: line $i+1$ is $z_i$.  Its SHA--256 digest is
\begin{center}
\texttt{5058792bf79dd594034393954aac53bf801a57f7a36e989c55efefc9c270fd50}.
\end{center}

and in the public repository
